Privacy Policy

NearbyJobs · Effective date: 5 October 2026

NearbyJobs ("we", "us") is a local job board app that connects employers with job seekers nearby. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data — when you sign in with Google or Facebook: your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Profile data you provide — phone number, store/company name and address (employers).
  • Job posts — title, description, salary, shift, contact phone, work address, the map location of the workplace and an optional storefront photo. Job posts are public.
  • Messages — conversations between job seekers and employers inside the app.
  • Payments — records of coin top-ups and spending (amount, product, time, transaction reference). Card or bank details are processed by Google Play, the App Store or your bank; we never receive them.
  • Device data — a push-notification token so we can notify you of new messages.

2. Your location

Job seekers' location is used only on your device to show jobs within the radius you choose. We do not store your location on our servers. Employers' location is captured once when posting a job to place the job on the map.

3. How we use data

  • To operate the service: show jobs, deliver messages, process coin top-ups and apply membership tiers.
  • To keep users safe: detect scams and abusive content (automated keyword checks, user reports and admin review).
  • To send notifications you can turn off in your phone settings.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Sharing

  • Public: job posts and the employer's store name are visible to all users.
  • Between users: messages are visible only to the two participants (and administrators when investigating a report).
  • Service providers: Supabase (database and hosting), Google and Facebook (sign-in), Google Play / Apple App Store and RevenueCat (in-app purchases), Expo (push notifications), and our bank-transfer notification provider. They process data only to provide their services.
  • Legal: when required by law or to protect users from fraud.

5. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. Payment records are kept as long as required for accounting and tax purposes. Job posts and messages are deleted when you delete your account, except where we must keep them for legal reasons or an open fraud investigation.

6. Your rights

You can view and edit your profile in the app, and you can request a copy of your data or deletion of your account by emailing us. We respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and protected by row-level access rules so users can only access their own private data. No system is 100% secure; please never share passwords or pay any "fee" requested by an employer.

8. Children

NearbyJobs is not intended for users under 16.

9. Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date above.
